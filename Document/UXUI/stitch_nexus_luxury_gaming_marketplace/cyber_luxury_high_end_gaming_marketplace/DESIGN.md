## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an ultra-premium "Cyber-Luxury" aesthetic engineered specifically for high-stakes, elite video game asset and account acquisition. It bridges the precision, high-tech velocity of competitive esports and cyberpunk digital environments with the bespoke, velvet-rope exclusivity of high-end private banking and haute horlogerie.

### Target Audience & Emotional Response
The target demographic consists of high-net-worth gamers, pro players, elite collectors, and digital asset connoisseurs who demand absolute provenance, security, and prestige. The interface evokes feelings of:
- **Exclusivity & Provenance:** Every listing feels like a vaulted artifact, not mere inventory.
- **Paramount Security:** Heavy visual anchors, structural gold framing, and tactical cryptographic finishes induce instant trust.
- **Futuristic Power:** Neon cyber glows juxtaposed against deep, light-absorbing voids give users the tactile thrill of commandeering an advanced terminal.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Archetype
The style blends **Cyber-Glassmorphism** and **Tactile Cyber-Brutalism with Opulent Finishes**:
- **Deep Light Traps:** Backgrounds utilize impenetrable obsidian tones to maximize luminescence from layered gold accents and cybernetic highlights.
- **High-Jewelry Detailing:** 1px precision metallic hairpins, directional gold gradients, and micro-chamfers mimic machined metal chassis and laser-cut circuit traces.
- **Glassmorphic Shields:** Frosted, low-opacity slate plates equipped with multi-layered specular reflections and hardware-accelerated backdrops establish spatial depth without visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette leverages a deep void anchored by refined precious metals, electrical energy accents, and surgical status indicators.

### Key Roles
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37` / Metallic Gold):** Denotes supreme tiering, transaction triggers, verified ownership badges, and high-value metric emphasis. Used with a directional luminous gradient (`#FFDF00` -> `#D4AF37` -> `#996515`) on hero interactive elements.
- **Secondary Accent (`#8A2BE2` / Cyber Violet):** Drives secondary visual paths, rare and mystical tier indicators, cryptographic stamps, and tertiary action fills.
- **Tertiary Accent (`#00F0FF` / Neon Cyan):** Reserved for high-voltage interactive states, focus rings, real-time activity indicators, telemetry streams, and cursor magnetic fields.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#0B0E14` / Deep Obsidian):** The foundational vacuum canvas. Absorbs extraneous light and maximizes screen contrast.
- **Neutral Surface (`#151A23` / Frosted Slate):** Used for elevated containers, interactive cards, and overlays. Semi-transparent at `0.75` to `0.85` alpha with subtle gold boundary traces (`rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`).

### Functional & Semantic Colors
- **Verified / Vault Safe:** Emerald Green (`#10B981`) for instant fraud-check approval, escrow confirmations, and seller security ratings.
- **Warning / Escrow In-Progress:** Amber (`#F59E0B`) for conditional steps, cooldown periods, and transfer verifications.
- **Danger / Critical:** Crimson (`#EF4444`) for authentication alerts, irreversible transfer confirmations, and system blocks.

## Typography

The typographical hierarchy pairs technological angularity with crystalline readability:
- **Headings & Badges (`Space Grotesk`):** Provides a sharp, high-tech, cyber-futuristic structural feel while maintaining proportional elegance. Uppercase styling is mandatory for `label-sm` and `label-md` tokens to evoke cryptographic tags and militaristic inventory coding.
- **Body & Data Displays (`Inter`):** Offers high-density legibility across complex multi-attribute account statistics, transaction ledgers, inventory matrices, and financial tables.
- **Numerical Alignment:** Monospaced numerical formatting (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) must be applied across all account stat callouts, prices, wallet balances, and countdown clocks to ensure zero-jitter layout reflows during dynamic updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a strict 12-column fluid grid system bounded by high-density horizontal and vertical guidelines, reflecting tactical HUD blueprints:
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12 columns, fixed maximum canvas container of 1440px, 24px gutters (`gutter`), 40px outer canvas margins (`margin`).
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8 columns, fluid full-bleed, 20px gutters, 24px outer margins.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, dynamic viewport-fill, 12px gutters (`gutter-mobile`), 16px outer margins (`margin-mobile`).

Vertical rhythmic pacing operates along an immutable 8px base grid, collapsing to 4px solely for micro-data points, cryptographic verification chips, and badge internal padding. Layout compartments prefer dense, modular grouping rather than sprawling whitespace, maximizing the feel of an elite tactical control center.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through layered translucent strata, illuminated borders, and directional kinetic lighting rather than traditional drop shadows.

### Elevation Hierarchy
1. **Canvas Base (Level 0):** Pure `#0B0E14` void with an optional SVG nano-mesh pattern at 3% opacity.
2. **Elevated Surfaces / Cards (Level 1):** `#151A23` tinted with 80% alpha, backed by a `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` layer. Outlined with a continuous 1px solid hairline: `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus (Level 2):** `#151A23` at 92% alpha with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Ambient drop: `0 8px 32px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`, paired with a top-lit gold hairline `rgba(255, 223, 0, 0.3)`.
4. **Modal Vaults & Transaction Shields (Level 3):** Grounded by a multi-tiered shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95)`, accompanied by a diffuse outer perimeter glow of Neon Cyan: `0 0 40px -8px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15)`.

### Shimmer & Tilt
- **3D Interactive Tilt:** Marketplace asset cards leverage transform perspective (`perspective: 1000px`) combined with subtle pointer-tracking rotation (max ±4deg).
- **Luminous Shimmer:** Top borders of elevated containers feature an animated linear-gradient pseudo-element that simulates a passing metallic glint along the 1px edge.

## Shapes

The design system embraces a **Soft-Chiseled Cybernetic** geometry (`roundedness: 1`):
- Base interactive elements (buttons, inputs, listing cells) feature a tight 4px (`0.25rem`) radius, preserving a precision-engineered, military-grade industrial edge.
- Mid-sized structural components (cards, dialogs, inspection frames) use 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Full structural modules (escrow summaries, verification dossiers) step up to a maximum of 12px (`0.75rem`).
- Standard circular styling (`rounded-full`) is strictly restricted to user avatar discs, multi-factor security pulses, and status dot pings. Continuous UI elements must never appear playful or organic.
- Accent containers occasionally introduce 45-degree micro-chamfered corners (using linear-gradient clipping paths) on primary high-tier inventory showcases to invoke reinforced armor plating.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Sovereign (Vault Action):** Background is a continuous 135-degree linear metallic gradient from `#FFDF00` through `#D4AF37` to `#996515`. Black text (`#0B0E14`), Space Grotesk bold, uppercase tracking. Hover triggers a dynamic inner gold drop-reflection and a glowing Cyan focus border.
- **Secondary Cybernetic:** `#151A23` surface, 1px border of `rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.5)` (Cyber Violet). Hover triggers full violet back-fill at 20% opacity with a `box-shadow: 0 0 16px rgba(138, 43, 226, 0.4)`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Transparent fill, metallic gold hairline typography, hover brings an obsidian frosted panel into view (`rgba(21, 26, 35, 0.6)`).

### Cards (Asset & Listing Showcases)
- Built on `#151A23` at 80% opacity with `16px` backdrop blur.
- Encased in a 1px border of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- Hover triggers a smooth elevation shift: border color transitions to `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.6)` with an ambient cyan under-glow (`0 12px 24px -6px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.12)`).
- Top right features an embedded tier-level hex badge; bottom features instantaneous tabular metrics (Win Rate, Skins Total, Escrow Guaranteed).

### Chips & Rarity Tags
- Height: 24px. Border-radius: 4px.
- Background: `rgba(11, 14, 20, 0.75)` with a 1px colored keyline matching asset tier:
  - **Relic / Immortal:** Gold (`#D4AF37`) with animated gold shimmer text.
  - **Apex / Cyber:** Cyber Violet (`#8A2BE2`) with purple luminescence.
  - **Verified Clean Asset:** Emerald (`#10B981`) background tint with an accompanying static lock glyph.

### Form Inputs & Search Arrays
- Height: 44px. Background: `#0B0E14` (recessed into the `#151A23` card).
- Border: 1px `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- Text: Inter, `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder: `#151A23` lightened to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.3)`.
- Focus State: Zero native outline; 1px Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`) perimeter border with an immediate `0 0 10px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.3)` glow. Space Grotesk label floats above with high-tracking uppercase.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Rigid 16px squares (checkbox) or octagons (radio).
- Default: Recessed obsidian center, 1px slate-white border.
- Selected: Gold (`#D4AF37`) fill with a dark void checkmark, throwing an active micro-glow of `0 0 8px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.5)`.

### Specialized Component: Escrow Verification Seal
- High-security visual anchor for asset checkout pages.
- Segmented dual-ring border with spinning radar track in Neon Cyan (`#00F0FF`).
- Central shield emblem stamped with Emerald (`#10B981`) cryptographic hash string in monospaced micro-type.
